WHAT THIS TOOL IS FOR

See how a crypto investigation works

Crypto transfers can be hard to understand when they are just a list of numbers and addresses. This demo turns one real, saved USDT case into a picture you can explore. USDT is a cryptocurrency, and an address is where it can be sent or received.

Click an address to see its transfers, open the supporting transaction details, and read the investigation report. The goal is to show what happened, what might explain it, and what we still do not know.

When would I use this?
  • Use it to learn how an analyst follows crypto transaction evidence.
  • Use it to review a concrete example of investigative reasoning.
  • No setup needed: the example is already loaded.

This is one saved case, not a live search tool. Connections and similar transfer amounts do not prove wrongdoing or identify the people involved.

Provenance & limits

Raw JSON-RPC requests and responses are saved in data/raw. SHA-256 checks detect local changes against the manifest; they are not independent proof of blockchain inclusion. Block hashes are checked against the saved node responses. The node remains a trusted data source.
